The Logistics Problem

How do e-commerce services fulfill orders efficiently across multiple centers?

  • Proximity Overhead: Finding the closest active warehouse to minimize shipping distances and latency.
  • Fulfillment Splitting: Safely splitting orders between at most 2 warehouses to control shipping costs when single warehouses are short.
  • Capacity Limits: Managing daily order handling caps. Full warehouses must route overflow to next-closest centers.
  • Safety Inventory: Tracing low-stock limits and triggering automated replenishment alerts on-the-fly.
